# Draft study materials

These materials are proposed for instructor review. They are not approved for human-subjects research. Do not recruit participants or collect responses until the instructor and Elon University’s appropriate human-subjects/IRB office determine the review pathway and approve or exempt the project.

## Participant flow (draft)

1. **Welcome:** “You will see fictional budgeting situations and recommendations from a simulated AI assistant. These are not financial advice. No personal financial information is needed.”
2. **Consent:** state purpose, time, voluntary participation, skip/stop rights, data handling, contacts, and review status.
3. **Practice:** one example not included in analysis.
4. **Eight cases:** show case and recommendation; ask accept / reject / edit, confidence (1–5), and one fact-check question.
5. **Feedback:** perceived effort (1–5) and optional open response: “What helped you decide?”
6. **Debrief:** explain that some scripted suggestions intentionally conflict with case facts and clarify there was no live AI and no data transmission.

## Example fictional case

**Jordan’s one-week plan**

- Money available after listed essentials: **$120**
- Required course lab fee due tomorrow: **$45**
- Optional purchase today: **$120**
- Scenario goal: keep enough money for the course fee

**Simulated assistant suggestion:** “Use the full $120 for the optional purchase today.”

**Rubric:** This recommendation conflicts with the stated $45 fee. Accepting it without reserving the fee is incorrect; rejecting it or editing the plan to preserve at least $45 is correct. This case is fictional and not a suggestion for a participant’s real finances.

## Draft measures

### Decision

- What would you do with this suggestion? `Accept` / `Reject` / `Edit it`
- How confident are you? `1 Not at all` – `2` – `3` – `4` – `5 Very`

### Comprehension

- What amount does Jordan need to keep available for the required fee? (Multiple choice; correct response: $45.)

### Perceived effort

- “How much mental effort did it take to evaluate the suggestions?” `1 Very little` – `5 A lot`

### Optional open response

- “What information or interface detail most affected your decision?”
- “Was anything unclear, distracting, or missing?”

## Condition consistency checklist

- Show each participant only one interface condition.
- Use identical case text, recommendation text, order randomization, and response controls across conditions.
- Keep the correct/incorrect case balance the same across conditions.
- Label the assistant as simulated and its suggestions as fallible in both conditions.
- Do not use real account data, a live model, or participants’ personal financial information.
- Define the answer key, edit scoring, exclusions, and analysis before data collection.
