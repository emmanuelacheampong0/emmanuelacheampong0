Product · Brand · Engagement Case Study

Building Voima's Brand & Digital Product Presence

Emmanuel Sefah Acheampong, Social Media & Engagement Lead, Voima

Selected work in brand system design, digital campaigns, product communication, early-user recruitment, and engagement analytics for Voima, a youth-led sickle-cell initiative building an AI companion app for SCD management.

01The Challenge

Voima works across education, advocacy, research, community engagement, technology, and digital health. The brand needed a consistent identity that could communicate trust and hope while making complex health information accessible.

The communication problem

Create a recognizable system that could work across social media, presentations, fundraising, partnerships, awareness campaigns, and product-facing communication.

The product opportunity

Support the emerging Voima App with clear, user-friendly communication around daily check-ins, symptom patterns, tailored guidance, community support, and early-user testing.

02My Role & Working Model

My contribution sat between brand, product communication, digital engagement, and execution, translating organizational goals into assets and campaigns that people could understand and act on.

Brand system

Developed a 15-page brand guide covering logo use, color, typography, imagery, voice, applications, governance, and marketing.

Product communication

Created product-facing communication for the Voima App and early-user test recruitment.

Campaign execution

Designed awareness, educational, fundraising, and community-facing digital campaigns.

Engagement

Used platform analytics and audience response to understand reach and guide content decisions.

Cross-functional coordination Product positioning Go-to-market communication User acquisition Stakeholder alignment Metrics & iteration

Designed to keep Voima consistent across channels while communicating strength, dignity, accessibility, and hope.

04Product Launch & Early-User Recruitment

The Voima App creative translated an emerging digital-health product into a simple user story: check in, understand patterns, receive tailored guidance, and access community support.

Go-to-market contribution

Positioned the app around everyday sickle-cell management, created launch communication, promoted the early-user test program and waitlist, and used a direct call to action to move awareness toward participation.

Product thinking

Connected product features to user needs, turning internal product ideas into external communication that potential users could understand quickly.

05Campaigns Across the User Journey

The content system supported multiple goals at once: educate, build trust, recruit users, and mobilize support.

Awareness

Health observance content designed to make sickle-cell and blood-donation messages visible and shareable.

Mobilization

Fundraising communication that explains the mission and gives supporters a direct path to act.

Education

Accessible myth-busting and educational content designed for nontechnical audiences.

07What I Learned

Consistency is a product decision.

A brand system reduces friction: teams know how to communicate, and users encounter a more coherent experience.

Features need translation.

Users do not experience a feature list; they experience whether a product makes sense for their life.

Engagement is feedback.

Analytics are useful when they inform the next content or communication decision, not when they are treated as vanity metrics.

Leadership is coordination.

Strong execution depends on turning broad goals into clear deliverables, aligning people, and moving work from idea to publication.
